# Library browsers

Open with **Ctrl+L**. The tabs cover:

- **boards**;
- **component models**;
- **code libraries**;
- **datasheets**.

Type to search; use the filters to narrow by interface, status, platform or manufacturer.

## Seed data in this build

The entries come from the Phase 0 surveys and are marked **"seed (Phase 0, not yet
qualified)"**. A board, model or part becomes usable in a design only after it passes its
qualification suite. Boards are qualified in Increment 2, the parts catalogue in Increment 4 and
component models in Increment 5.

## Datasheets

Most manufacturers do not allow their datasheets to be redistributed. EmbedForge therefore
ships a reviewed **parameter sheet** with the datasheet's address. Raspberry Pi Ltd documents
are the exception and are bundled unchanged. You can import datasheet files you downloaded
yourself; they stay on this computer and are never copied into projects or shared packages.
