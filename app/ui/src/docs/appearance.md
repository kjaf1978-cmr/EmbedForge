# Themes and font size

- **Theme:** light, dark, or follow the system setting (Settings, or **Ctrl+Shift+L**).
- **Font size:** from 80 % to 200 % in steps of 10 %, with **Ctrl+=** and **Ctrl+-**;
  **Ctrl+0** resets to 100 %.
- **Small screens:** at 1366×768, split views are off and the side panes open as overlays.
  Nothing is removed: every function stays reachable through menus and the command palette.
